The three problems

The parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out with day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names, and most of the reports.

The booking-v3 form hint says MM/DD/YYYY. The form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first, but tells people to type the month first. They do what the hint says, and the booking lands swapped. It only comes through the form, only for tenants on booking-v3. Fixing the parser does not fix it: the hint (or how the form reads its input) has to change.

The date moves by one day. “Tuesday showed as Monday.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday.” A day/month swap cannot do that. The desk already lists these in desk-notes.json as read as a one-day move; they look like a time zone conversion bug. They stay counted under the parser too, where the desk filed them.

How to tell them apart

  • Day and month swapped, report from the app or email: parser.
  • Day and month swapped, report from the form of a booking-v3 tenant: form hint.
  • Same month, one day off, or different days in different places: one-day move.
  • The reporter’s browser locale (intake’s tag) does not tell you the cause. The tenant does.

Lessons

  • Matching on subject is fast, but “the date is wrong” is a symptom, not a cause. Before a ticket is marked known, read a sample of what was merged into it.
  • The export pages overlap: count reports by id, once.
